WHY THIS EXISTS

  

    
--- their_memory.txt
+++ souvenance.me

    
- Native AI memory: written without you, stored at the lab, non-portable. Their lock-in.

    
+ Yours: auditable SQL on your server. Leave any provider, keep every memory.

    
- Legacy apps: memory for others, after you. A printed book, a posthumous profile.

    
+ Memory for you, alive: query it daily. It still becomes a dynamic autobiography.

    
- Agents that act first, remember badly, and notify you constantly.

    
+ The silence rule: it never calls you back. Deep when you come; silent when you leave.
