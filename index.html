PROJECT SOUVENANCE — a memory that belongs to its subject.

The AI labs already keep a memory of you: 96% of ChatGPT memories are written by the system, on their servers. That file is why you can’t leave. Souvenance inverts the experiment: your memory lives on YOUR server, and the AI only visits.

How it works

No diary. Forward a voice note, talk to Claude — two taps, kept forever. At 03:00 it sorts your day while you sleep: keeps what matters, forgets the noise, dates every change of mind without ever deleting one. Three weeks later, in Claude: “what did I decide about the house?” — it answers with your own past, sources included, down to the original recording in your own voice.

Why you can trust it

Memory apps live on your attention; this one has no notification code at all. And the exit is built in: the export is plain SQL and ordinary files, the AI is a swappable adapter. Stop paying: a pause, never a loss.

FAQ

How do I own my AI memory instead of a lab owning it?

Run the memory layer yourself: append-only PostgreSQL on your VPS, nightly consolidation, served to Claude through MCP. The SQL dump is the export.

What does it cost to run?

About zero beyond a small VPS — the AI layer uses whatever access you already have, or runs fully local with Ollama.

What if I stop paying — or the project dies?

Capture never depends on the AI layer; consolidation catches up later. Plain SQL, markdown and original files — readable in 30 years.

Is my memory private?

Your server, your reverse proxy, an executable constitution, a full audit log, no outbound actions in v1.

Will it nag me with notifications?

Never — structurally. Deep when you come, silent when you leave.

What do I need?

A Linux VPS (2 vCPU / 4 GB for the core — size to the local bricks you enable), Docker, a domain, and an AI backend (Claude CLI, an API key, or any model via Ollama) or API key.
